# E-Commerce Exploratory Data Analysis (EDA)

## Project Overview
This project analyzes the Olist Brazilian E-Commerce dataset to understand business performance, order trends, product category revenue, customer satisfaction, and delivery performance.

## Dataset
Source: Brazilian E-Commerce Public Dataset by Olist.

The dataset includes:
- Orders
- Order items
- Products
- Product category translations
- Customers
- Customer reviews

## Tools and Technologies
- Python
- Pandas
- SQL (SQLite)
- Matplotlib
- Google Colab
- GitHub

## Project Objectives
- Analyze order status and fulfillment rates.
- Identify monthly order trends.
- Identify top revenue-generating product categories.
- Understand customer geographical distribution.
- Analyze the relationship between delivery time and customer satisfaction.

## Key Findings
- 97.02% of orders were successfully delivered.
- Only 0.63% of orders were canceled.
- Monthly order volumes increased significantly throughout 2017.
- Health & Beauty generated the highest revenue of approximately R$1.45 million.
- Watches & Gifts and Bed, Bath & Table ranked second and third in revenue.
- São Paulo, Rio de Janeiro, and Minas Gerais had the highest customer counts.
- Delivery time and customer review scores showed a negative correlation of -0.33.

## Business Recommendations
- Improve delivery speed and reliability in high-demand states.
- Work with logistics partners to reduce delivery delays.
- Invest in high-performing product categories.
- Investigate categories with high sales volumes but lower customer review scores.

## Conclusion
Olist demonstrates strong order fulfillment and customer demand. Improving delivery efficiency and focusing on customer satisfaction may support future business growth.
